Source Glance is installed

  1. Open Source Glance: press ⌘ Space and type “Source Glance”, or find it in the Applications folder in your home folder.
  2. The first time, it opens on Accounts. Sign in to GitHub or GitLab.
  3. Your issues, pull requests and projects then show in the menu bar, at the top right of your screen.

Your sign-ins are kept in your login Keychain, never in a file.
